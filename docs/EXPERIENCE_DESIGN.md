# Experience Design — GHE Metaverse

## Fantasy
Learners explore a ForgeX renewable energy campus and train as Green Hydrogen specialists. Module 1 inducts them as a **Hydrogen Explorer**.

## Session goals (Module 1)
Duration target: 12–20 minutes.

1. Orientation on the campus hub
2. Four interactive stations mapped to learning outcomes
3. Knowledge check (ForgeX quiz)
4. Badge ceremony + completion video

## Learning outcomes → stations

| Outcome | Station | Mechanic |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Explain what hydrogen is | Meet Hydrogen | Inspect H₂ molecule + fact markers |
| Hydrogen bonding / H₂O | Bonding Lab | Assemble two H onto O |
| Why green + electrolysis | Green Electrolyzer | Connect renewables → electrolyze → observe bubbles |
| Energy carrier | Energy Carrier Map | Sun → Electricity → H₂ → truck/ship/factory |

## Interaction model
- **Desktop/web:** WASD move, Shift+drag look, click to interact
- **Quest VR:** Enter VR button → WebXR controllers, ray pointers, teleport
- **Accessibility:** Reduce-motion toggle, large HTML panels, Field Manual PDF link

## Tone
Playful ForgeX voice (friendly quiz distractors) with clear industrial-clean visuals: sky blue, leaf green, white plant infrastructure.
